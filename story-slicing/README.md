# story-slicing

Story slicing framework grounded in Gary Straughan's "Development That Pays" perspective — *Software Development As If Profit Mattered*.

## What it does

Helps you slice user stories into shippable, valuable increments. Grounds every slicing decision in commercial utility rather than process ritual.

## Core ideas

- **Slicing replaces estimation** — the valuable part of planning poker is the discussion, not the number. Slicing captures the discussion and produces better output.
- **Ticket count as forcing function** — measure velocity in tickets completed (not story points) and teams self-organise toward small stories. Aligned with Vasco Duarte's #NoEstimates.
- **The car analogy** — a story needs both *direction* (steering: what value does this deliver?) and *power* (pedal: is it small enough to move?). Estimation only addresses the pedal.

## When to invoke

- "Is this story too big?"
- "We spent two hours estimating and got nowhere"
- "How do we slice this?"
- "Planning poker feels broken"
- "How do we forecast without story points?"
